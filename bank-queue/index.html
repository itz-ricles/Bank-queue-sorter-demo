Bank Queue System

An application of queues (first in, first out). The queue logic is written in C++ (BankQueue.cpp) and runs in your browser as WebAssembly.

      Inside the arrays

      Each lane is a circular queue in a fixed array of 10 slots. F is the front (next to be served). R is the rear (next free slot). When R reaches the end it wraps to slot 0.

      Take a ticket = enqueue at the rear, O(1). Call next = dequeue from the front, O(1). Nobody is shifted; the front index just moves on. Estimated waits come from walking the line, O(n).
